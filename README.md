# Life at 8x, redesigned

Hi, I'm **Hassaan**. 👋

This is my take on the "Life at 8x" page. I had **one hour**, one old page to understand, and one reference site to learn from. (Yes, the clock was ticking the whole time.)

**To see it:** open `index.html`. No installing, no setup. If you can double-click, you can view it.

---

## The idea

The old page listed facts. I wanted you to *feel* them.

So I kept what worked, dropped what didn't, and told the story in eight short parts, one scroll. It looks and moves like 8x, so it feels like the same place.

---

## What's inside

- A short intro message
- 250,000+ people, shown as dots you can count
- What 8x believes, with a ring that changes as you scroll
- Team videos, and a "would I fit?" check
- The founders, and open roles you can filter
- A dotted **8x** at the bottom that reacts to your mouse (or your phone tilt)
